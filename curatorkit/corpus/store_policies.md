# Retail Store Operations & Customer Service Policies

## Section 1: Returns, Refunds, and Exchanges

### Clause RET-01: Standard 30-Day Return Window
All unopened, sealed items in original packaging can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery for a 100% full refund to the original payment method. Proof of purchase (Order ID or receipt) is required. [POL-RET-01]

### Clause RET-02: Opened Electronics & Restocking Fee
Opened electronics, computing equipment, and mobile devices in like-new condition with all original accessories are eligible for return within 15 calendar days of delivery. A standard 15% restocking fee applies to open-box returns unless the product arrived defective or damaged. [POL-RET-02]

### Clause RET-03: Non-Returnable Merchandise
The following categories are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable once shipped:
1. In-ear earphones and earbuds (due to health and hygiene safety standards).
2. Digital software activation keys, gift cards, and downloadable licenses.
3. Custom-configured or personalized devices.
Defective in-ear audio is covered exclusively under warranty replacement. [POL-RET-03]

### Clause RET-04: Damaged or Defective on Arrival (DOA)
Items that arrive physically damaged or functionally dead-on-arrival must be reported within 48 hours of delivery receipt. We provide an immediate free prepaid shipping label and dispatch a replacement unit at zero cost. [POL-RET-04]

## Section 2: Warranty & Product Protection

### Clause WAR-01: Standard Manufacturer Warranty
Every new hardware product purchased through the store includes the standard 1-year manufacturer limited warranty covering hardware defects in materials and workmanship. Consumables (such as batteries experiencing normal capacity decay) and accidental liquid/drop damage are not covered under standard warranty. [POL-WAR-01]

### Clause WAR-02: Extended Protection Plan (CareShield)
Customers who purchase the optional CareShield 2-Year or 3-Year Protection Plan within 30 days of hardware purchase receive 100% parts and labor coverage, including up to two claims for accidental drops and spills with a flat $49 repair deductible. [POL-WAR-02]

### Clause WAR-03: Refurbished & Open-Box Warranty
Certified Refurbished and Open-Box products undergo rigorous 30-point inspection and include a 90-day comprehensive store replacement guarantee. [POL-WAR-03]

## Section 3: Shipping, Delivery & Tracking

### Clause SHIP-01: Free Standard Shipping Tier
Orders with a merchandise subtotal of $50.00 USD or greater qualify for Free Standard Ground Shipping (3 to 5 business days transit time). Orders under $50.00 incur a flat $5.99 shipping fee. [POL-SHIP-01]

### Clause SHIP-02: Express & Overnight Shipping
Expedited 2-Day Air shipping is available at checkout for a flat $12.99 fee. Overnight Priority Delivery (next business day delivery for orders placed before 2:00 PM EST) is available for $24.99. [POL-SHIP-02]

### Clause SHIP-03: International Shipping & Customs
We currently ship to over 45 countries via DHL Express. All international orders are delivered Duties & Taxes Paid (DDP); local import tariffs are calculated at checkout so no fees are collected at delivery. [POL-SHIP-03]

## Section 4: Pricing, Price Matching & Promotions

### Clause PRICE-01: 14-Day Price Match Guarantee
If a qualifying competitor (such as Best Buy, Amazon Direct, or B&H Photo) lowers the advertised price for an identical, in-stock SKU within 14 days of your purchase date, we will refund the difference to your original payment method. [POL-PRICE-01]

### Clause COUP-01: Promotional Codes & Discount Enforcement
Customer service agents and AI shopping assistants have no authority to generate custom percentage discounts or override listed prices. Only officially published promotional codes (e.g., WELCOME10 for 10% off your first order, or TECH50 for $50 off orders over $500) may be redeemed at checkout. Unauthorized discounts or voucher manipulation will be rejected. [POL-COUP-01]
