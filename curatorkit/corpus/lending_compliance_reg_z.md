# Standard Operating Procedure: Consumer Lending & Fair Lending Regulations
**Document ID:** POL-LEND-2026-V3  
**Regulatory References:** Truth in Lending Act (TILA / Regulation Z - 12 CFR Part 1026), Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA / Regulation B - 12 CFR Part 1002)

---

### Section 201: Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z) Disclosures
201.1 Loan Estimate (LE) Timing Requirements:
Within three (3) general business days after receiving an application for a consumer residential mortgage loan, the bank must provide the applicant with a compliant Loan Estimate (Form H-24). The bank cannot assess any fees (other than a bona fide and reasonable fee for obtaining a credit report) until the applicant has received the Loan Estimate and indicated an intent to proceed.

201.2 Closing Disclosure (CD) 3-Day Rule:
The consumer must receive the final Closing Disclosure (Form H-25) at least three (3) specific business days prior to consummation of the transaction. If the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) increases by more than 1/8 of 1% (0.125%) for regular loans, or 1/4 of 1% for irregular loans, a revised Closing Disclosure must be issued, resetting a new mandatory 3-business-day waiting period.

201.3 Right of Rescission:
For credit transactions secured by the consumer's principal dwelling (such as refinances and home equity lines of credit, but excluding initial purchase-money residential mortgages), the consumer has the right to rescind the transaction until midnight of the third business day following:
- Consummation of the transaction;
- Delivery of all required material TILA disclosures; or
- Delivery of the required notice of the right to rescind, whichever occurs last.
No loan funds may be disbursed until the rescission period has lapsed without revocation.

### Section 202: Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B)
202.1 Prohibited Bases for Credit Decisions:
A creditor shall not discriminate against any applicant on a prohibited basis regarding any aspect of a credit transaction. Prohibited bases include:
- Race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age (provided the applicant has the capacity to contract);
- The fact that all or part of the applicant's income derives from any public assistance program;
- The fact that the applicant has in good faith exercised any right under the Consumer Credit Protection Act.

202.2 Adverse Action Notification Requirements:
Within thirty (30) calendar days after receiving a completed credit application, the bank must notify the applicant of its approval, counteroffer, or adverse action (denial). If adverse action is taken, the notice must:
- Be in writing;
- Disclose the specific reasons for denial (or inform the applicant of their right to receive the reasons within 60 days upon written request);
- Provide the required ECOA notice of non-discrimination and supervisory agency contact details.

202.3 Inquiries Regarding Marital Status:
For individual, unsecured credit, a creditor shall not inquire into the applicant's marital status (such as inquiring if an applicant is married, single, or divorced) unless the applicant resides in a community property state or relies on community property assets.
