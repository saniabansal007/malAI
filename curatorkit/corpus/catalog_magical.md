# Enchanted Emporium --- Magical Product Catalog

> **Dataset note:** Enchanted Emporium is a fictional wizarding
> e-commerce store created for chatbot development. Prices, SKUs, stock
> levels, product specifications, ratings, and retail features below are
> invented catalog data, not official Harry Potter merchandise claims.
> Lore-inspired names are used for the fictional setting. The store uses
> Galleons (G) as its fictional catalog currency; no real-world exchange
> rate is implied.

## Catalog conventions

-   Every SKU is unique.
-   Prices are fictional and expressed in Galleons (G).
-   Ratings and review counts are illustrative synthetic data.
-   "In stock" is a synthetic snapshot for this dataset, not live
    inventory.
-   House robes are separate products, each with its own SKU and
    house-specific support scenarios.
-   The Ravenclaw robe listing uses **blue and bronze** trim for this
    catalog.
-   Product features marked as enchanted or magical are fictional retail
    specifications for this dataset.
