# Standard Operating Procedure: Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
**Document ID:** POL-AML-2026-V4  
**Regulatory References:** Bank Secrecy Act (31 U.S.C. 5311), USA PATRIOT Act Section 326, FinCEN CDD Rule (31 CFR 1010.230)

---

### Section 101: Customer Identification Program (CIP)
101.1 Minimum Required Information:
Before establishing any customer account or credit relationship, bank personnel must obtain, verify, and document the following four pieces of identifying information:
- Full legal name (as stated on official government identification)
- Date of birth (for natural persons)
- Physical residential or principal business address (P.O. Boxes are strictly prohibited without an accompanying physical street address)
- Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), Social Security Number (SSN), or Employer Identification Number (EIN). For non-US persons, an unexpired passport number and country of issuance is required.

101.2 Documentary Verification:
Verification must occur within 5 business days of account opening using primary unexpired government-issued photo identification (e.g., State Driver's License, US Passport, State ID). If the identification is expired, illegible, or appears altered, account opening must be suspended immediately.

101.3 Beneficial Ownership Requirements (Legal Entities):
For accounts opened on behalf of legal entities (corporations, LLCs, partnerships), bank staff must identify and verify:
- Each individual who directly or indirectly owns 25% or more of the equity interests of the entity.
- One single individual with significant responsibility to control, manage, or direct the legal entity (e.g., CEO, CFO, Managing Member).
Failure to provide beneficial ownership certification requires immediate rejection of the onboarding request.

### Section 102: Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) & Structuring
102.1 Currency Transaction Report (CTR) Threshold:
A FinCEN Form 112 Currency Transaction Report must be filed for each deposit, withdrawal, exchange of currency, or other payment or transfer by, through, or to the bank which involves a transaction in currency of more than $10,000 in aggregate across all related accounts in a single business day.

102.2 Prohibition of Structuring:
Under 31 U.S.C. 5324, it is a federal crime for any person to structure, or assist in structuring, or attempt to structure or assist in structuring, any transaction with one or more domestic financial institutions for the purpose of evading CTR reporting requirements.
Bank representatives are strictly prohibited from:
- Advising any customer on how to conduct transactions below the $10,000 threshold to avoid reporting.
- Suggesting splitting deposits into amounts such as $9,500 or $9,900.
- Warning customers that a transaction will trigger a CTR filing.

### Section 103: Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR) & Tipping-Off Prohibition
103.1 SAR Filing Mandate:
A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed with FinCEN no later than 30 calendar days after the date of initial detection of facts that may constitute a basis for filing. If no suspect was identified on the date of detection, the bank may delay filing for up to 60 calendar days to identify a suspect.

103.2 Absolute Confidentiality ("No Tipping Off"):
Under 31 U.S.C. 5318(g)(2), federal law strictly forbids any financial institution, director, officer, employee, or agent of any financial institution from disclosing to any person involved in the transaction, or any third party, that a SAR has been reported, filed, or considered.
Violating the tipping-off prohibition carries severe civil penalties and criminal imprisonment up to 5 years. Any inquiry by a customer asking if their account has been reported must be met with standard neutral processing language without confirming or denying SAR activity.

### Section 104: Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) & Sanctions (OFAC)
104.1 OFAC Screening:
All parties involved in any transfer or account relationship must be real-time screened against the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) and Blocked Persons list prior to transaction execution. Any positive hit requires immediate asset blocking and notification to the AML Compliance Officer within 2 hours.

104.2 Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs):
Foreign or domestic senior political figures, their immediate family members, and close associates require Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) and Senior Executive Management approval before onboarding. Source of Wealth (SoW) and Source of Funds (SoF) documentation is mandatory.
