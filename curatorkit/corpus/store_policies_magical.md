# Enchanted Emporium --- Store Operations & Customer Service Policies

> **Dataset note:** Enchanted Emporium is a fictional wizarding
> e-commerce store. All prices, deadlines, fees, remedies, and service
> commitments in this file are invented rules for chatbot training. They
> are not official Harry Potter canon or the policies of any real
> retailer.

## Policy interpretation rules

1.  Apply the specific policy for the product and issue first; apply a
    general policy where no specific exception exists.
2.  Do not invent exceptions, discounts, delivery dates, stock
    availability, or refunds.
3.  If a required fact is missing (such as delivery date, order number,
    item condition, or whether personalization was applied), ask a
    concise follow-up question.
4.  If two policies appear to conflict, do not promise an outcome.
    Explain the uncertainty and escalate to a human support agent.
5.  The four house robes are separate SKUs. House-specific rules below
    apply only to the named robe. General apparel and returns rules
    still apply when not contradicted.
6.  For all returns, the order ID or proof of purchase is required.

## Section 1: Returns, Refunds & Exchanges

### Clause RET-01: Standard Unopened Return Window

-   Unopened, unused items in original packaging may be returned within
    30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund to the original
    payment method.
-   Proof of purchase is required.
-   The item must include all supplied accessories, inserts, and
    packaging.
-   Refund processing begins after the returned item is received and
    inspected.

### Clause RET-02: Opened General Merchandise

-   Opened non-personalized merchandise may be returned within 15
    calendar days of delivery if it is in like-new condition with all
    included parts.
-   A 10% restocking fee applies to discretionary opened-item returns.
-   The restocking fee does not apply if the item arrived damaged,
    defective, or materially different from its listing.
-   This clause does not override specific exclusions for personalized
    goods, consumables, or fragile items with missing parts.

### Clause RET-03: Personalized Products

-   Personalized or name-embroidered items are not eligible for
    change-of-mind returns once personalization has begun.
-   If the store made a personalization error, or the item arrived
    damaged or defective, the customer may request a correction or
    replacement at no cost.
-   Support must verify the order's personalization details before
    promising a remedy.

### Clause RET-04: Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Item on Arrival

-   Customers should report visible shipping damage, missing components,
    or an incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery.
-   Support should request the order ID and a brief description;
    photographs may be requested for physical damage or incorrect
    markings.
-   For verified damage, defect, or store picking error, the store
    provides a prepaid return method and offers a replacement or full
    refund.
-   If the product is limited stock and a replacement is unavailable,
    offer a refund rather than promising a restock date.

### Clause RET-05: Non-Returnable Items

-   Personalized items are excluded from change-of-mind returns once
    personalization begins.
-   Opened stationery consumables and used decorative seals are not
    returnable for change of mind.
-   Items damaged by misuse, unauthorized alteration, or failure to
    follow care instructions may be ineligible for a free replacement.
-   A defect or incorrect item supplied by the store must still be
    reviewed under RET-03 or RET-04.

### Clause RET-06: Exchange Process

-   Size or variant exchanges are subject to stock availability.
-   Customers must provide the order ID and identify the desired size,
    house, or variant.
-   If the requested replacement is unavailable, offer a refund under
    the applicable return rule.
-   Do not claim that an exchange is confirmed until inventory has been
    checked.

## Section 2: House-Specific Robe Policies

The following four robe products are separate catalog entries: -
Gryffindor House Robes --- `MAG-ROB-GRY-001` - Slytherin House Robes ---
`MAG-ROB-SLY-001` - Ravenclaw House Robes --- `MAG-ROB-RAV-001` -
Hufflepuff House Robes --- `MAG-ROB-HUF-001`

### Clause ROB-GRY-01: Gryffindor Crest Missing or Incorrect

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-GRY-001` only.
-   If the robe arrives without the Gryffindor lion crest, or with
    another house's crest, treat it as an incorrect or defective item
    under RET-04.
-   After verifying the order, offer a no-cost replacement. If no
    replacement is available, offer a full refund.
-   Do not ask the customer to pay return shipping for a verified store
    error.

### Clause ROB-GRY-02: Gryffindor Color or Gold Embroidery Defect

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-GRY-001` only.
-   The listing specifies scarlet and gold lining.
-   If the delivered robe materially differs from those listed colors,
    or gold embroidery is damaged on arrival, handle it under RET-04.
-   Normal variation in screen display alone does not establish a
    defect; ask for a photo or product description if the difference is
    unclear.
-   Change-of-mind returns remain subject to RET-01 or RET-02.

### Clause ROB-SLY-01: Slytherin Crest or Green/Silver Trim

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-SLY-001` only.
-   The listing specifies an emerald-green and silver trim and Slytherin
    serpent crest.
-   A missing or incorrect crest, or a material listing mismatch on
    arrival, qualifies for review under RET-04.
-   Verified store errors qualify for a free replacement or full refund
    if replacement stock is unavailable.
-   Do not apply the Gryffindor crest policy as if it were a
    Slytherin-specific product rule.

### Clause ROB-SLY-02: Slytherin Personalized Embroidery

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-SLY-001` only.
-   Name-personalized robes are not eligible for change-of-mind returns
    after personalization begins, under RET-03.
-   If the name, spelling, or placement differs from the customer's
    confirmed order, offer a corrected replacement at no cost.
-   Ask the customer to confirm the spelling shown on the order before
    deciding whether the issue is a store error.

### Clause ROB-RAV-01: Ravenclaw Crest and Blue/Bronze Listing

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-RAV-001` only.
-   This catalog listing specifies blue and bronze trim and a Ravenclaw
    eagle crest.
-   A missing/incorrect crest or delivered trim that materially differs
    from the listing qualifies for review under RET-04.
-   Customers seeking a blue-and-silver film-style version should be
    informed that this SKU is the blue-and-bronze version. If the
    customer ordered the listed version and simply changed their
    preference, apply the normal return rules rather than treating it as
    a defect.
-   If the store sent a different variant than the one ordered, treat it
    as an incorrect item under RET-04.

### Clause ROB-RAV-02: Ravenclaw Size and Sleeve Exchange

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-RAV-001` only.
-   Customers may request a size exchange within 30 days if the robe is
    unused and in resalable condition.
-   Opened/try-on condition is assessed under RET-02; an exchange is
    subject to stock availability under RET-06.
-   A robe with incorrect sizing labels or dimensions materially
    different from the listing should be reviewed as a potential listing
    or fulfillment error under RET-04.

### Clause ROB-HUF-01: Hufflepuff Crest or Yellow/Black Color Defect

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-HUF-001` only.
-   The listing specifies yellow and black trim and a Hufflepuff badger
    crest.
-   Missing/incorrect crest, torn lining, or visible discoloration on
    arrival qualifies for review under RET-04.
-   Verified defects receive a no-cost replacement or a full refund if
    replacement stock is unavailable.
-   Discoloration that occurs after use or cleaning contrary to the
    garment label is not automatically a delivery defect and requires
    review.

### Clause ROB-HUF-02: Hufflepuff Personalized Robes and Gift Sizing

-   Applies to SKU `MAG-ROB-HUF-001` only.
-   Personalized robes follow RET-03: no change-of-mind return once
    personalization begins.
-   If a gift recipient needs a different size, an unused
    non-personalized robe may be exchanged under RET-06, subject to the
    applicable return window and stock.
-   If the store embroidered the wrong name or sent the wrong size
    compared with the order, offer a free correction or replacement
    after verification.

### Clause ROB-ALL-01: Shared House Robe Rules

-   Applies to all four robe SKUs.
-   Standard sizes are XS--XXL, but actual availability must be checked
    before confirming an exchange.
-   A robe's house crest and trim must match its specific product
    listing.
-   A customer who ordered the wrong house may request an exchange under
    RET-06; the exchange is not automatic and depends on condition,
    timing, and stock.
-   House affiliation alone does not create different refund deadlines.
    The specific rules above address product identity, color,
    embroidery, and sizing; general return rules still apply to other
    cases.

## Section 3: Warranty & Product Protection

### Clause WAR-01: Standard Product Guarantee

-   New physical merchandise includes a 90-day store guarantee against
    manufacturing defects.
-   The guarantee covers defects present under normal intended use.
-   It does not cover ordinary wear, accidental damage, misuse,
    unauthorized modification, or damage caused by ignoring product care
    instructions.
-   This is a fictional store guarantee, not a claim about any
    real-world manufacturer's warranty.

### Clause WAR-02: Premium Item Review

-   The Invisibility Cloak collectible, Nimbus 2000 Broomstick, and
    Undetectable Extension Trunk require order verification before a
    replacement or refund is authorized.
-   The review may include photos of shipping damage, packaging, or the
    item.
-   Review does not mean the customer's claim is denied; support must
    explain the next step and avoid promising a result before
    verification.
-   The Invisibility Cloak is a display collectible and does not provide
    actual invisibility.

### Clause WAR-03: Fragile Product Handling

-   The Remembrall and Divination Crystal Ball are fragile decorative
    products.
-   Damage reported within the 48-hour arrival window is handled under
    RET-04.
-   Damage caused after delivery by dropping, impact, or improper
    handling is not automatically covered by the store guarantee.

## Section 4: Shipping, Delivery & Tracking

### Clause SHIP-01: Standard Shipping

-   Orders with a merchandise subtotal of 20 G or more qualify for free
    standard fictional courier delivery.
-   Orders below 20 G incur a 1 G delivery fee.
-   Standard delivery estimate: 3--5 business days after dispatch.
-   The estimate is not a guarantee. The chatbot must not invent live
    tracking events or claim a parcel has dispatched without order data.

### Clause SHIP-02: Express Delivery

-   Express delivery is available for an additional 3 G where offered at
    checkout.
-   Estimated transit time is 1--2 business days after dispatch.
-   Availability depends on destination and item handling requirements.
-   If express delivery is not shown on the order, do not promise that
    it can be added after dispatch.

### Clause SHIP-03: Tracking Requests

-   To check an order, request the order ID and the email or account
    reference used at checkout.
-   If live tracking information is not available in the chatbot's data,
    explain that limitation and direct the customer to the store's
    order-support channel.
-   Never fabricate tracking numbers, courier scans, delivery attempts,
    or estimated arrival dates.

### Clause SHIP-04: Shipping Damage or Missing Package

-   Physical damage should be reported within 48 hours of delivery under
    RET-04.
-   If tracking says delivered but the customer cannot locate the
    parcel, ask the customer to check the delivery location and
    household/receiving desk, then escalate for a courier trace.
-   Do not issue a refund or claim the package is lost until the trace
    or store procedure supports that decision.

### Clause SHIP-05: Special Packaging

-   Broomsticks, fragile display items, and premium artifacts are packed
    with protective materials where available.
-   Packaging type does not guarantee that an item cannot be damaged in
    transit.
-   A customer should report damage using RET-04.

## Section 5: Pricing, Promotions & Discounts

### Clause PRICE-01: Catalog Price

-   The catalog price is the listed price at the time the order is
    placed.
-   Prices in this training dataset are fictional Galleon values.
-   The chatbot must not convert Galleons to real-world currency unless
    a separately approved conversion rule is supplied.

### Clause PRICE-02: Price Adjustment

-   If the store's listed price for the same SKU falls within 7 calendar
    days of purchase, a customer may request a price adjustment.
-   The item and order must be verified; limited-time bundles,
    personalized products, and clearance items are excluded.
-   If approved, refund the difference to the original payment method.
-   A lower price on a different SKU or variant does not qualify.

### Clause COUP-01: Published Promotions Only

-   Only promotions explicitly listed in the current store data may be
    applied.
-   Customer-service agents and the chatbot cannot invent a discount,
    waive a fee, or manually override a price.
-   If no active promotion data is available, say that the chatbot
    cannot verify a current offer and direct the customer to checkout or
    human support.
-   Do not invent coupon codes.

## Section 6: Order Changes, Cancellations & Stock

### Clause ORD-01: Order Cancellation

-   Customers may request cancellation before dispatch.
-   Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled through
    this policy; the customer may request a return under RET-01 through
    RET-06, as applicable.
-   A cancellation is not confirmed until the order status has been
    checked.

### Clause ORD-02: Address or Variant Changes

-   Address, size, house, and personalization changes may be requested
    before dispatch, but are not guaranteed.
-   For robe changes, verify the exact robe SKU and requested
    house/size.
-   Do not silently change a Gryffindor order to Slytherin, or any other
    house, without the customer's explicit confirmation.
-   If the order has already been dispatched, explain that changes may
    no longer be possible and offer the appropriate next step.

### Clause INV-01: Stock Accuracy

-   "In stock" values in the catalog are synthetic snapshot data for
    training.
-   In a real conversation, live inventory must be checked before
    confirming availability.
-   For limited-stock products, do not promise a restock date unless one
    is explicitly available.
-   If a replacement is unavailable for a verified store error, offer
    the refund option under RET-04.

## Section 7: Product Questions & Safe Expectations

### Clause PROD-HELP-01: Product Compatibility and Features

-   Use only features stated in the product catalog.
-   If a feature is not specified, do not infer that the product
    supports it.
-   Ask a clarifying question when the customer's requested variant or
    use is unclear.

### Clause PROD-HELP-02: Fictional Magical Features

-   Magical product descriptions belong to the fictional setting of this
    store.
-   The Invisibility Cloak collectible does not render its wearer
    invisible; the Marauder's Map collectible does not track real
    people; the Sneakoscope novelty does not verify trustworthiness; the
    crystal ball is decorative and does not guarantee predictions; and
    Floo Powder is a prop that does not transport anyone.
-   Do not present fictional features as real-world capabilities or
    promise real-world safety based on them.

### Clause PROD-HELP-03: Product Care

-   Recommend the care instructions included with the specific product.
-   If care instructions are unavailable, do not invent a specialized
    spell, chemical, or repair method. Offer escalation to product
    support.

## Section 8: Escalation & Service Recovery

### Clause ESC-01: Human Support Required

Escalate when: - The order record, delivery status, or inventory is
unavailable and a decision depends on it. - Two policies appear to
conflict. - A customer disputes a previous refund, replacement, or
charge. - A premium-item claim needs verification. - The requested
remedy is not covered by the written rules. - A customer asks for an
exception that the chatbot is not authorized to approve.

### Clause ESC-02: How to Respond During Escalation

-   Acknowledge the customer's issue politely.
-   State what information is needed and why.
-   Summarize the applicable policy without overstating certainty.
-   Do not claim that a refund, replacement, cancellation, or escalation
    has been completed unless a connected system confirms it.
-   Do not request passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

### Clause ESC-03: No Unauthorized Promises

-   The chatbot cannot create custom discounts, guarantee a delivery
    date, promise unavailable inventory, or bypass a stated return
    restriction.
-   It may explain eligible options and collect information for a human
    agent.
-   Use calm, respectful language even when the customer is frustrated.
